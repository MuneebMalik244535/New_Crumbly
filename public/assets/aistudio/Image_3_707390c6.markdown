## Brand & Style

This design system positions the brand at the intersection of haute couture editorial design and artisanal culinary indulgence. The brand personality is unapologetically decadent, poised, and tactile. It evokes the sensory delight of warm bakery aromas combined with the rigorous aesthetic refinement of high-fashion print monographs.

The target audience comprises discerning culinary enthusiasts, luxury gift buyers, and aesthetic-driven digital consumers seeking an elevated ritual rather than a casual confection. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Editorial High-Contrast Luxury**: Expressive serif titling juxtaposed against pristine, functional sans-serif metadata.
- **Warm Culinary Immersion**: Generous cream-based negative space framing deep chocolate and burnt-caramel hues.
- **Asymmetric Tension**: Off-center hero containers, staggered product showcases, and structural grid alignments evoking bespoke magazine spreads.
- **Subtle Tactility**: Organic physical cues paired with razor-sharp editorial borders and ultra-diffused, warm-tinted depth.

## Colors

The palette balances intense roasted cocoa undertones with velvety milk and cream tones, creating an appetite-stimulating yet sophisticated environment.

- **Primary (`#2B1712` - Deep Chocolate Brown)**: The anchor pigment. Used for primary typography, authoritative structural strokes, high-emphasis button fills, and primary branding moments.
- **Secondary (`#B8753C` - Caramel)**: The focal accent. Used for interactive states, key highlight tags, active badges, and promotional indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8A5237` - Milk Chocolate)**: The structural bridge. Used for secondary metadata, subtle hover states, tertiary buttons, and decorative architectural rules.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFF7EC` - Warm Cream)**: The primary ambient backdrop. Replaces cold digital whites with an unbleached parchment warmth.

### Functional Roles & Accents
- **Surface Elevation Tone (`#F4E8D6`)**: Tinted parchment for secondary card surfaces and elevated containers.
- **Muted Accent / Surface Border (`#E8D2B5` - Biscuit Beige)**: Structural dividing lines, ghost boundaries, and input borders.
- **Deep Surface Alt (`#4A2921` - Rich Cocoa)**: Inverted dark sections, luxury modal drawers, and footer blocks.
- **Text On Primary & Dark**: `#FFF7EC` (Warm Cream).
- **Text On Light**: `#2B1712` (Deep Chocolate Brown).
- **Secondary Copy**: `#684B3D` for muted narrative clarity.

## Typography

The typographical voice bridges high-contrast editorial elegance and contemporary digital utility.

- **Headlines & Display (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Commands attention with dramatic vertical stress and sharp bracketed serifs. Headings must remain uncluttered, frequently paired with generous bottom spacing. Use sentence case for narrative headlines and Title Case for product hero collections.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers warm, humanist clarity that balances the sharpness of Bodoni Moda. The wide open apertures ensure high legibility against tinted creamy backdrops and small ingredient callouts.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows**: Set `label-sm` and `label-md` in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to ground luxury metadata, pricing codes, and batch numbering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open-format editorial lookbook. Rather than homogeneous modular grids, the system relies on an expressive 12-column asymmetric fluid grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Content reflows vertically into sequential stacked stories. Visual cards lean into full-bleed horizontal scrolls.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces side-by-side pairings of macro food photography and narrative copy.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width `1440px`, `margin-desktop: 4.5rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`. Enables offset column spans (e.g., 5-column product image paired with an offset 6-column narrative block, with 1 column of pure negative air).

### Rhythm
Generous macro-spacing (`space-xl` and above) surrounds section dividers to establish luxury breathing room, while micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) clusters ingredient labels, taste profiles, and pricing tightly against related elements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows or harsh multi-tiered elevations. Depth is cultivated organically via **tonal surface layering**, **biscuit-beige hairline structural boundaries**, and **warm cocoa-tinted ambient diffusion**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Base Ground (`#FFF7EC`)**: Default viewport canvas. Flat, raw texture.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#F4E8D6`)**: Primary recipe cards, filter sidebars, and ingredient pods. Distinct via tonal fill, bounded by `1px solid #E8D2B5`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Modals (`#FFF7EC` / `#2B1712`)**: Cart draws, batch configurators, and quick-add modals. Elevated using a low-velocity, chocolate-tinted ambient cast:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(43, 23, 18, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 23, 18, 0.04);`
4. **Interactive Focus**: Element hover triggers a micro lift accompanied by a warm expansion:
   - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -6px rgba(43, 23, 18, 0.12);`

No sharp, uncolored black drop shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,...)`) are permitted in any state.

## Shapes

The system adopts a crisp, tailored aesthetic: level `1` (Soft). Geometric silhouettes mimic the bespoke packaging of artisanal bakeries—structured gift boxes, embossed ribbons, and foil-pressed tickets.

- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Bound to `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radii.
- **Cards, Drawers, and Image Portals (`rounded-lg`)**: Bound to `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radii.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Pure pill forms (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for dietary tags (e.g., "Gluten-Friendly", "Single-Origin Cru") and floating counter indicators to create dynamic contrast against architectural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Chocolate (`#2B1712`) fill with Warm Cream (`#FFF7EC`) typography (`label-lg`). Border: `1px solid #2B1712`. Corner radius: `0.25rem`. Transitions smoothly to Rich Cocoa (`#4A2921`) on hover with subtle upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Accent**: Caramel (`#B8753C`) fill with `#FFF7EC` text. Reserved for primary purchase triggers ("Order Fresh Batch").
- **Tertiary (Outline/Ghost)**: Transparent background, `1px solid #2B1712`, Deep Chocolate text. On hover, fills with Biscuit Beige (`#E8D2B5`) at 30% opacity.
- **Editorial Text Link**: Underlined serif action, utilizing Bodoni Moda (`headline-sm`) accompanied by a subtle directional arrow icon.

### Chips & Badges
- **Taste & Dietary Badges**: Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `6px 14px`, text `label-sm`. Background `#F4E8D6`, text `#4A2921`, border `1px solid #E8D2B5`.
- **Limited Release / Seasonal Chips**: Solid Milk Chocolate (`#8A5237`) background, Warm Cream (`#FFF7EC`) text, zero border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls**: Background `#FFF7EC`, height `48px`, border `1px solid #E8D2B5`, padding `0 1rem`. Typography `body-md` in `#2B1712`. Placeholder text in `#8A5237` with 60% opacity.
- **Focus State**: `1px solid #B8753C` with an ambient glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 117, 60, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `18px`, `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Border: `1.5px solid #8A5237`.
- Checked state: `#2B1712` fill with a crisp `#FFF7EC` check glyph.

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards**: Surface `#F4E8D6`, hairline border `1px solid #E8D2B5`, `rounded-lg` (`8px`). Visuals are framed by an inner warm padding (`space-sm`) to emulate matted gallery prints.
- **Hover State**: Border shifts smoothly to `#B8753C` with a warm ambient elevation lift.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Flavour Profile Gauge**: A delicate multi-axis horizontal spectrum illustrating notes (e.g., "Bittersweet", "Buttery", "Fudgy") using `#E8D2B5` track lines and `#B8753C` indicators.
- **Bespoke Box Builder (Assortment Selector)**: A modular grid container with dashed `#8A5237` placeholder slots that snap to visual cookies and pastries as the consumer curates their bespoke dozen.
- **Tasting Notes Drawer**: A sliding edge panel bathed in Rich Cocoa (`#4A2921`) with Warm Cream (`#FFF7EC`) typography detailing origin cacao percentages, allergens, and baker notes.
